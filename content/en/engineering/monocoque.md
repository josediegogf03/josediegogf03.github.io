Contribution to the manufacturing and assembly of the **100% carbon fiber monocoque chassis** of the EcoVolt CCM vehicle.

### Goals
- Optimize **structural rigidity**
- Reduce **overall weight**

### Context
The vehicle competes in **Shell Eco-marathon** and reached about **200 km/kWh** at a speed of about 28 km/h.
