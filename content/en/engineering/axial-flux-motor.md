Team project to develop and validate the design of an **axial-flux traction motor** for a Shell Eco-marathon-type vehicle (Tec NextGen Scientist challenge), within the constraints of the energy storage system and the inverter. The project is **in progress**.

### Approach
- Electromagnetic and thermal design in **Ansys Motor-CAD**
- Mechanical design in **SolidWorks**
- A parametric study to find the most influential variables, followed by **multi-objective optimization** (torque, efficiency, mass, electrical and thermal constraints)
- Prototype manufacturing in a later phase

### Reference
Hanselman, *Brushless Permanent-Magnet Motor Design*.
